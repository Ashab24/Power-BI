## 🚀 Featured Project: Retail Sales & Inventory Analytics

An end-to-end Power BI project built around a realistic retail dataset, designed to demonstrate how business requirements can be translated into interactive dashboards and analytical solutions.

### Business Objectives

- Monitor overall sales performance and revenue trends.
- Analyze month-over-month (MoM) and year-over-year (YoY) growth.
- Compare current-year sales with previous-year performance.
- Evaluate employee sales against monthly targets.
- Analyze product returns and category-level return rates.
- Monitor inventory quantities and valuation across warehouses.
- Identify inventory concentration and potential data-quality issues.
- Enable business users to investigate performance through interactive filters and dynamic rankings.

### Key Features

- **Executive Sales Overview:** KPI cards, monthly sales trends, category contribution, and store performance.
- **Sales Performance & Growth Analysis:** MoM growth, YoY comparisons, and category contribution to sales changes.
- **Employee Performance:** Target achievement, sales variance, and performance classification.
- **Inventory Dashboard:** Inventory valuation, closing units, and warehouse-level comparisons.
- **Inventory Analysis:** Product-level inventory investigation and stock-related exceptions.
- **Inventory Concentration:** Pareto analysis and cumulative inventory value.
- **Dynamic Top/Bottom N Analysis:** User-controlled ranking, dynamic visual titles, and interactive filtering.

## 💡 My Development Approach

I follow a business-first approach to analytics development:

1. Understand stakeholder requirements and define business KPIs.
2. Inspect, transform, and validate source data.
3. Design a reliable data model based on the grain and relationships of each table.
4. Develop reusable DAX measures and validate their behavior.
5. Build intuitive, interactive dashboards with a consistent design system.
6. Test calculations, slicers, visual interactions, and edge cases.
7. Document business definitions, assumptions, limitations, and future improvements.

I also use AI tools to accelerate development and troubleshooting while independently validating calculations and business outcomes.
